# How to diagnose outbound - animated infographic brief

## 1. Post context

**Source post (full text):**
```
Email is most certainly not “dead.”
It just became harder to get results with average execution.

If you want outbound that gets replies, you must understand this:
You are no longer just fixing your copy.

There’s more than one reason outbound fails. And fixing the wrong one won’t help.

This is how I see it after working with 125+ B2B companies:

1️⃣ Setup is how you get delivered.
→ If your emails land in spam, nothing else on this list matters.
→ Use separate sending domains, warmed inboxes, and check where emails actually land instead of assuming.

Do this today: count how many domains and mailboxes you send from, who warms them, and whether inbox placement is checked or just assumed.

2️⃣ Signal is how you get a reason to send.
→ Every send needs a relevant signal behind it, not just a name on a list.
→ Signal-triggered outbound gets 1-3% positive replies. Generic outreach gets 0.2-0.5%.

3️⃣ Message is how you get read.
→ Use the words your customers actually use, not a template nobody has touched in months.

Do this today: find where your current copy came from and when it was last changed. If nobody knows, rewrite it based on the insights from a recent customer calls.

4️⃣ Offers and lead magnets are how you get replies
→ Create something of value that is frictionless and easy to consume
→ Focus on surfacing pains rather than providing complete solutions

Do this today: read Alex Hormozi on what a good leadmagnet is.

5️⃣ Outbound works when all 4 work together.
→ Often, more than one part is broken. Fixing just one may not be enough.
→ The campaign runs for weeks, replies stay low, and the team blames the channel instead of diagnosing the problem.

Do this today: Check your reply rate and how you measure it.

a. Industry average on cold audiences: 2.43%
b. Well-targeted and warm campaigns: 5–8%
c. Top 10%: 10.7%+

Get all 4 right, and you turn outbound into a repeatable source of meetings.

Ekipa went from zero replies overnight to 15-30 sales meetings a week.
If your results aren’t moving, stop blaming the channel. Find what’s broken across all three.

Do you know which of the 4 is your weakest?

If not, find out before you send another message.
```

**Post type:** Framework Breakdown

**Audience:** B2B founder or VP of Sales with a low-reply outbound campaign.

**Why this post needs a visual:** The graphic makes the four possible failure points quick to scan and diagnose.

### Content

| SETUP | Delivered? | Lands in spam | Check inbox placement, domains, and mailboxes. |
